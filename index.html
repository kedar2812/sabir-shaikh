What's on the crest

It was drawn as a logo. It turned into a set of rules. Keep scrolling and I'll walk you through them.

1 of 4

The tiger

Nerve. The tiger is for the conversations most people put off: the hard negotiation, the honest review, the call to the client who is three months late. Whoever walks in first usually sets the terms.

2 of 4

The horse

Stamina. Horses have been a passion of mine for years, and they teach what business does: nobody wins on the first furlong. Most of what I've built came from turning up on ordinary days.

3 of 4

The pen and the book

If I can't explain a decision on one page, I'm not ready to make it. The pen is for writing it down. The book is for reading what everyone before me already worked out.

4 of 4

The flags

Ground taken, and held. Two flags, because nothing worth holding is held alone. Some of the people at SAVIANS have been with me for more than twenty years.

All four at once

Nerve without stamina burns out. Stamina without thinking goes in circles. The crest is there to remind me to carry all of it.

About Sabir. Curious about ideas.people.places.conversations. ideas, people, places and conversations.

Sabir Salim Shaikh is the founder, chairman and managing director of SAVIANS Group, based in Pune. The group works across architecture, interiors, branding, construction and project execution, and employs more than 2,000 people.

He learns the way most good operators do: from the people he meets, the places work takes him and the ideas he tries out. Away from the site, you'll find him on horseback or at the shooting range.
